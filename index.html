SHARED KITCHEN & BATHROOM RULES

Please follow these simple rules to keep our shared spaces clean, hygienic, and comfortable for everyone.

▼
🍳
RULE 1

KEEP THE KITCHEN CLEAN

Keep the kitchen clean and tidy at all times.

Immediately after finishing cooking:

  • Clean the kitchen countertop.
  • Clean the stove.
  • Clean all surfaces you have used.
  • Wash your dishes.
  • Put everything back in its proper place.
🧊
RULE 2

USE ONLY ONE REFRIGERATOR

⚠️ This is a VERY IMPORTANT RULE.

Each person is allowed to use ONLY ONE REFRIGERATOR for storing their personal food and belongings.

  • You must choose ONE refrigerator.
  • Keep all your food and personal items in that one refrigerator.
  • Do NOT store your belongings in two or more refrigerators.
  • Do NOT occupy space in multiple refrigerators.
  • Leave enough space for other residents.
🚿
RULE 3

FLUSH THE TOILET AFTER EVERY USE

🚽 FLUSH THE TOILET AFTER EVERY USE.
  • Always flush the toilet after every use.
  • Leave the bathroom clean and hygienic for the next person.
🤝
RULE 4

RESPECT SHARED SPACES

  • Do not leave dirty dishes.
  • Do not leave food waste.
  • Do not leave personal belongings in common areas.
  • Always clean up after yourself.
  • Respect other residents.
✨
RULE 5

BE RESPONSIBLE

These facilities are shared by everyone.

🌱 Your cooperation is essential to maintaining a clean, organized, and comfortable living environment.

THANK YOU FOR YOUR COOPERATION!

Please respect these rules and consider the comfort of others.

"A little care makes shared living better."
